<p class="about-lead">
I study how landscapes and near-surface environments evolve on Earth and across the Solar System. Landslides, avalanches, dunes, rivers, lakes, and sediment transport provide natural experiments for understanding how gravity, atmosphere, climate, volatiles, and surface materials control planetary surfaces.
</p>

<p>
I combine planetary mission data, remote sensing, field observations, image processing, environmental seismology, and physics-based numerical modeling. Other worlds extend the range of conditions available on Earth and help test which geological and climate processes are universal.
</p>
